CrownFoundry Privacy Policy

Effective 20 August 2026

  • App: com.surenjanath.crownfoundry
  • Developer: Surenjanath Singh
  • Contact: surenjanath.singh@gmail.com

CrownFoundry is a draughts (checkers) game. It has no accounts, no advertising, and no third-party analytics or crash-reporting libraries of any kind.

The short version

If you play offline, nothing leaves your phone. The opponent, the referee, your match history, the post-game review and the puzzles all run on the device.

If you connect the app to a CrownFoundry server, it sends the moves of that game and a random identifier the app generated for itself, so the opponent can remember how you play. It never learns who you are, because it is never told.

What is collected

Only when the app is connected to a CrownFoundry server, and only what a game of draughts consists of:

DataWhat it isWhy
Player IDA random identifier generated by the app on first launch. Not your device ID, not an advertising ID, and not derived from any hardware value.So the opponent can adapt to one player across games instead of treating every match as a stranger.
Game movesThe moves of each match, the board positions they produced, and their timestamps.The server referees the game, so it must know the position. It is also what match history and post-game analysis are built from.
Match resultsWins, losses, draws, captures, length, chosen difficulty and rule variant.Your record, the learning-curve charts, and the difficulty adaptation.
Playing styleTwo derived numbers describing how aggressively you trade pieces and how quickly you push for kings.They set the opponent's search depth and risk appetite — this is what makes it adapt to you.

The app requests two Android permissions, INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, and nothing else.

What is never collected

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account — there is no sign-up.
  • Location, of any precision.
  • Contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera.
  • Advertising identifiers, or any hardware identifier such as IMEI, MAC address or Android ID.
  • Crash reports or usage analytics — the app contains no analytics SDK.
  • Anything at all from Pass and Play games, which never leave the device.

Playing offline

CrownFoundry ships with its opponent built in, so the game is fully playable with no connection and no server. In that mode no data leaves your device.

If you later connect to a server, games you played offline are uploaded so your history is in one place. If you never connect, they never leave.

Who it is shared with

Nobody. The data is not sold, rented, traded, or shared with any third party. It is not used for advertising or for profiling beyond adapting the in-game opponent to your play.

It is stored on the CrownFoundry server the app is pointed at. If you run your own server, your data is on your own machine and this policy describes only the software's behaviour.

How to delete your data

Because there is no account, the app's Player ID is the only handle on your data. Everything held against it can be erased: the profile, every match, every stored position, and every record the opponent kept of those games.

  1. Find your ID — Settings → Backend → Identity → Player id. Tap it to copy.
  2. Request erasure — email surenjanath.singh@gmail.com with that ID, and it will be deleted.
  3. Or do it directly — the server exposes a deletion endpoint:

`` DELETE https://<your-server>/api/player/<your-player-id>/ ``

Deletion is immediate and permanent. There is no soft-delete and no backup copy retained for recovery.

Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the device, including offline match history and the on-device opponent.

How long it is kept

Match history is kept until you delete it, because it is the feature — your record and your learning curve are built from it. There is no other retention period and nothing is archived elsewhere.

Children

CrownFoundry is a game of draughts and is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age, because it does not collect personal information at all — no name, no email, no account.

Security

Traffic between the app and a server is sent over HTTPS. Published builds refuse unencrypted connections to anything other than a loopback address on the device itself.

Changes to this policy

If the data the app handles changes, this page changes with it and the effective date above is updated. Material changes are also noted in the app's release notes.
