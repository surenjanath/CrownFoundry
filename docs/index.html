What it does

It learns your habits

A reinforcement-learning engine adjusts how it values positions from the games you play, so it stops repeating the lines that lost.

Works with no connection

The trained opponent ships inside the app. Full-strength search on a plane, on the underground, or with data off.

Shows its reasoning

Every move comes back with the shortlist attached — what else it weighed, and by how much it preferred what it played.

Reviews your games

An evaluation curve, your accuracy against its accuracy, and the one move that decided the game.

Puzzles from your mistakes

The errors the review finds become practice positions — real positions you reached, with the move you could have played.

No ads, no tracking

No advertising network, no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no account. Nothing to sign up for.
